Privacy

Updated October 23, 2026

Tommy the Bernese is a public creator media kit. Visitors do not need an account to use this site.

Information shown on the site

With the account owner's authorization, an automated job reads Tommy's follower counts from connected Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube accounts; public YouTube view totals; recent Instagram content and aggregate post metrics. The site displays rounded audience figures, while the public site data file contains the source counts and selected post links, captions, and thumbnails. It does not access direct messages or publish on Tommy's social accounts.

Visitor information

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and understand how people find and use this site. It uses cookies and collects page views, approximate location (such as country, region, or city), device and browser details, referral sources, and interactions such as outbound link clicks. This information is sent to Google and reported to Tommy's team in aggregate; see Google's Privacy Policy. If you email Tommy's team, your message and email address are used to respond to your inquiry. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may process technical request data under its own policies. External services such as Google Fonts and Instagram may receive standard browser requests when their content loads.

Storage and sharing

Platform access credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are not published on the site. The public website data is stored in the site repository. We do not sell visitor information.

Your choices

You can visit social platform links without using this website. The account owner can revoke platform access through each platform's settings. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email tommythebernese@gmail.com.
